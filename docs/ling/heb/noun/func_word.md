# Finctional Words

## Definite Article ה

The base form of a noun is indefinite and there's no indefinite article in Hebrew. To make a noun definite, we need to add a definite article before the noun. Note that proper nouns are always definite by definition and do not need a definite article.

The basic form of the definite article is a הַ followed by the first consonant of the noun, written as a daghesh forte in the first consonant.

## Conjunctive ו
